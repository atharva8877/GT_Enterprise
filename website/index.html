INDUSTRIAL JOB WORK · TALAWADE, MAHARASHTRA

Precision-machined components and dependable job work, shaped around your specifications.

Established in October 2012, G.T. Enterprises is an industrial job work company based in Talawade, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Maharashtra. We deliver quality work tailored to customer drawings and requirements. Our capabilities span Grinding, Rolling, Automatic and CNC Traub machining, threading, thread rolling, and tapping. We work with Mild Steel (MS), Aluminium, Brass, Copper, Stainless Steel, and other metals, bringing precision workmanship, reliable service, and timely completion to every job.
